Usability test · Nov 2026

Testing kit: Solum App v2

A moderated, 40-minute session with one solo seller at a time. The prototype runs in a browser. Each task below has a link that opens the app at the right starting screen.

What we want to learn

  1. Can a new seller get from sign-up to their own outlined area without help?
  2. Is logging a visit and setting a follow-up fast enough to do between doors?
  3. Do sellers read the map language (white, olive, gold pins) without a legend?
  4. Do the Free limits feel fair, and is Plus at $8 a month a clear step up?

Who to recruit

Six to eight solo sellers, mixed across:

  • Door-to-door home services (solar, roofing, pest control)
  • Real estate agents who prospect by neighborhood
  • B2B route sellers who visit shops and offices

Screen for people who sell alone and visit at least 20 addresses a week. Note what they use today (paper, spreadsheet, another app).

Session outline

TimePart
0–5 minWarm-up. How do you plan a day in the field today? How do you remember who to go back to?
5–30 minTasks 1–7. Ask them to think aloud. Do not explain the screens.
30–37 minPlans and pricing questions.
37–40 minWrap-up. One thing to keep, one thing to change.

Tasks

Read the scenario aloud. Open the link before you hand over the device. Mark each task as done alone, done with a hint, or not done.

1. Get started
Open: welcome

“You just downloaded Solum. Set it up so you can start using it today.”

Done when they finish the sample-area walkthrough and save an outline of their own. Watch which plan they pick and whether they read the plan cards.
2. Who to see first
Open: map

“It’s 10:30 in the morning. Who should you visit first today, and how do you get there?”

Done when they find Grace Whitfield (11am) and open directions. Watch whether they swipe cards or tap pins, and if gold reads as “to see”.
3. Nobody answered
Open: map

“You knocked at 1569 SE Nehalem St and nobody was home. Record it so you remember to try again.”

Done when the visit is saved as Not home with the retry switch on. Watch whether they notice the retry switch without prompting.
4. Come back Friday
Open: map

“Maria Lopez asked you to come back next week with a quote. Set that up.”

Done when a follow-up for next week is saved with a note. Watch whether the time chips are enough or they want a calendar.
5. A house that isn’t on the map
Open: map

“You met Rosa Delgado at 1620 SE Spokane St, a new build. Add her as a contact.”

Done when Rosa is saved. Watch whether they tap the map first or look for a typed option, and if they find “Type the address”.
6. Fix a phone number
Open: contacts

“Tom and Priya Nguyen gave you a new number: (503) 555-0199. Update it.”

Done when the number is saved. Watch whether they search, sort, or scroll.
7. A new neighborhood
Open: settings (Free)

“Next week you want to work the streets south of Nehalem too. Get that set up.”

Done when they reach the limit sheet and decide. If they start a trial, let them outline the area. Watch their reaction to the limit.

Plans and pricing questions

Open the plans screen and ask:

  1. Which plan would you pick, and why?
  2. What would make Pro worth $16 a month to you?
  3. Would you pay yearly to save 25%?
  4. Which automation would you actually turn on?

Moderator notes

  • Reload the page between participants. The prototype keeps no data.
  • Directions, Call and Text are simulated. Say so if they ask.
  • The map shows Sellwood, Portland. Dragging works, pinch-zoom does not.
  • Run on a phone if you can. On a laptop, use the browser’s phone view.
